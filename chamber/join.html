Non-Profit Membership

Cost: Free ($0 / year)

  • Listing in the online Chamber Directory
  • Access to monthly community networking events
  • Inclusion in the monthly Chamber newsletter

Bronze Membership

Cost: $250 / year

  • All Non-Profit Membership benefits
  • 15% discount on Chamber workshops and training sessions
  • Ribbon-cutting ceremony announcement for new businesses

Silver Membership

Cost: $500 / year

  • All Bronze Membership benefits
  • 30% discount on Chamber events and booth sponsorships
  • Quarterly social media feature across Chamber channels
  • Access to specialized business leadership training

Gold Membership

Cost: $1,000 / year

  • All Silver Membership benefits
  • Rotating Spotlight position on the Chamber Home Page
  • 50% discount on all Chamber events and annual gala
  • VIP invitations to regional trade and policy roundtables
