Site Overview & Purpose

Site Name

Santa Fe Chamber of Commerce

Site Purpose

The Santa Fe Chamber of Commerce website serves as a digital platform to promote local commerce, connect entrepreneurs in Santa Fe, Argentina, provide a directory of verified local businesses, and share economic news and event notifications.

Target Audience

Local business owners, regional entrepreneurs, investors, consumers seeking reliable local service providers, and job seekers.

User Scenarios

  • A resident searches for a certified business in Santa Fe using the toggleable directory grid/list views.
  • A commercial member wants to evaluate membership tiers (NP, Bronze, Silver, Gold) and submit a registration application.
  • A user checks local real-time weather forecasts and upcoming commercial spotlights on the home page.

2. Color Palette & Visual Style

The color scheme combines Primary Navy with Terracotta and Gold accents to establish professional authority, warmth, and WCAG contrast compliance.

Primary Navy #0F2C59 Main headers, navigation backgrounds, dark section footers
Terracotta #C85A32 Subheaders, call-to-action highlights, category badges
Accent Gold #FFC436 Button states, spotlight borders, key interactive cues
Light Gray Surface #F8F9FA Page background, card background contrast

3. Typography Standard

Heading Font: Barlow

Barlow (SemiBold 600 / Bold 700) — Used for h1, h2, h3 headers, navigation links, and section titles.

Body Font: Montserrat

Montserrat (Regular 400 / Medium 500) — Used for body copy, business card attributes, form fields, and table metadata.

4. Sitemap & Page Structure

  • Home Page (index.html): SVG logo, responsive navigation bar, hero banner with join CTA, live OpenWeatherMap API widget, dynamic member spotlights.
  • Directory Page (directory.html): Interactive grid/list layout displaying commercial records fetched asynchronously from members.json.
  • Discover Page (discover.html): Responsive visual grid of local demographics, business sector growth, and lazy-loaded imagery.
  • Join Page (join.html): Structured HTML form for business onboarding with membership level descriptions and timestamp features.
