About Me

My name is Kevin Herrera, a 24-year-old student at BYU and a passionate software development enthusiast based in Santa Fe Capital, Argentina. Originally from Venezuela, I have a deep appreciation for Argentine culture, family, and hard work. As a dedicated member of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, I find great joy in serving others and staying actively involved in my community.

Professionally, I bring a strong background in customer service and operational problem-solving from my work as a ticket sales agent at the central bus terminal in Santa Fe (Empresa Río Uruguay). Beyond customer operations, I have hands-on experience in tech environments—including developing internal low-code web applications utilizing ToolJet and connecting databases—which has sharpened my logic, efficiency, and ability to build practical digital solutions.
